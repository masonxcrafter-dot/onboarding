# Weekly Client Call Agenda (15–20 min)

## 1. Opening (1 min)
> "Let's quickly go through what happened this week, what we're seeing, and what we're changing."

## 2. Review (5–7 min)
- Leads
- CPL
- Appointments
- Show rate
- Estimates
- Close rate
- Revenue
- Lead quality

## 3. Ask the Client (5–7 min)
- "How did the lead quality feel this week?"
- "Any common objections?"
- "Any services you're getting a lot of requests for?"
- "How's the follow-up process going?"

## 4. Next Week's Plan (3 min)
- What we're changing and why
- What we need from them

## 5. Log It
- [ ] Notes saved to `09 - Reports`
- [ ] Action items created as tasks with owners
- [ ] Any 🟢 Future requests logged for the 30-day review
