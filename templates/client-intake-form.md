# Client Intake Form

Build in **GoHighLevel, Typeform, Tally, or another form builder**. Map every answer to a GHL custom field where possible so it's visible on the client's contact record.

`*` = required

---

## Section 1 — Business Information
- Business name *
- Owner name *
- Email *
- Phone *
- Website *
- Business address *
- Service areas (cities / zip codes / radius) *
- Years in business *
- Number of employees *
- Number of salespeople *
- Number of technicians *

## Section 2 — Services
- What services do you currently offer? *
- Which service do you want to generate the most business for? *
- What is your average job value? *
- What is your highest-ticket service?
- What is your highest-margin service?
- Are there any services you do NOT want us advertising?
- **How many additional jobs can you handle per month?** *

> Capacity matters. We don't want to generate 200 leads for a company that can only handle 20 jobs.

## Section 3 — Offer
- What is your current offer?
- Do you currently offer discounts?
- Do you offer financing? (If yes: provider + terms)
- Do you offer free estimates?
- Do you offer free consultations?
- Do you offer warranties? (Details)
- Do you offer guarantees? (Details)
- What makes your company different? *
- Why should someone choose you instead of a competitor? *
- Do you have any current promotions?

## Section 4 — Ideal Customer
- Who is your ideal customer? *
- Residential, commercial, or both? *
- Typical homeowner age?
- Typical property value?
- Typical job type?
- Typical customer problem? *
- What usually causes someone to contact you?
- What objections do customers have?
- Why do customers choose you?
- What neighborhoods/cities produce your best customers?

## Section 5 — Sales Process
- Who answers incoming calls? *
- Who responds to leads? *
- How quickly do you normally respond? *
- What happens when a new lead comes in?
- How do you schedule estimates?
- How do you follow up with people who don't book?
- How many estimates do you typically complete per week?
- Approximately what percentage of estimates become jobs?
- What's your average close rate?
- What's your average job value?

## Section 6 — Current Marketing
Currently running? (checkboxes)
- [ ] Meta ads
- [ ] Google Ads
- [ ] TikTok
- [ ] Organic social
- [ ] Direct mail
- [ ] SEO
- [ ] Referral marketing
- [ ] Other: ____

Baseline numbers (these become the "Before" column in the 30-day review):
- Current monthly ad spend
- Current monthly leads
- Current booked appointments
- Current customers generated
- Previous agencies
- What worked?
- What didn't work?

## Section 7 — Access
Checkbox confirmation per platform — link the [Access Request Guide](access-request-guide.md).

> ⚠️ **Do not type passwords into this form.** We'll send invitations / partner requests for each account.

## Section 8 — Assets
Link to the upload folder + the [Asset Request List](asset-request-list.md).

## Final
- If we look back 90 days from now, what would make you say this was absolutely worth it?
- Anything else we should know?
