# Landing Page Outline

## Above the Fold
- **Headline:** `[Desired Outcome] Without [Major Pain Point]`
  - e.g. *"A Brand-New Roof Without the Runaround"*
- **Subheadline:** service + location + offer
  - e.g. *"Trusted roof replacement for homeowners in [City]. Get a free inspection and same-week quote."*
- **CTA button:** **Get Your Free Estimate**
- Trust bar: ★ rating + review count · years in business · licensed & insured · financing available
- Hero image/video: real team or real finished job

## Sections
1. **Problem** — name the pain the homeowner is feeling
2. **Solution** — how the client solves it
3. **Services** — primary service first
4. **Why Choose Us** — 3–4 differentiators from the Growth Brief
5. **Reviews** — real Google reviews with names + city
6. **Before / After** — real project photos
7. **Process** — 3 steps: *Request estimate → We inspect & quote → We do the job*
8. **Financing** — if offered
9. **FAQ** — answer the top objections from the kickoff call
10. **CTA** — repeat the offer + form

## Requirements
- Mobile-first; sticky click-to-call button on mobile
- Form above the fold on desktop, one tap away on mobile
- Page loads in < 3 seconds
- SMS consent + privacy policy link under the form
- Pixel + tracking on page and thank-you page
