# Kickoff Call Script

**Duration:** 30–45 minutes
**Objective:** Get enough information to build the marketing system without repeatedly bothering the client.

---

## 0. Open (2 min)
> "Thanks for making the time. The goal today is for us to really understand the business, your customers, and how leads turn into jobs on your end — so we can build everything without having to keep bugging you. I've gone through your onboarding form, so I'll mostly be filling in gaps."

## 1. Business (8 min)
> "Give me the quick version of the business. How did you get started and what do you guys primarily do?"

- What services make you the most money?
- What's your average job worth?
- What's your capacity? How many more jobs could you take on per month?
- What areas do you want more work from?

## 2. Customer (8 min)
> "Tell me about your best customers."

- What problem are they dealing with?
- Why do they call you?
- What makes them choose you?
- What are their biggest concerns?
- What objections do they have?

## 3. Offer (7 min)
> "If someone is ready to buy today, what can we give them a reason to contact you right now?"

Discuss:
- Free estimates
- Promotions
- Financing
- Guarantees
- Priority scheduling
- Seasonal offers

## 4. Sales (8 min)
> "Walk me through what happens from the moment a lead comes in."

Map it out live:

```text
Lead → Contact → Qualification → Appointment → Estimate → Close
```

For each step: **who** does it, **how fast**, **what tool**, and **what happens if it doesn't work** (no answer, no-show, estimate not accepted).

## 5. Goals (5 min)
> "If we look back 90 days from now, what would make you say this was absolutely worth it?"

📝 **Write their answer down word for word.** This is what we report against at the 30-day review.

## 6. Expectations & Next Steps (5 min)
- Point of contact: "I'm your main contact — everything comes through me."
- Response times: business hours within [4] hours, urgent within [1] hour
- What we need from them: any missing access/assets, approval on creative, and **fast lead response once live**
- Target launch date: [DATE]
- Weekly report day + weekly call time: [DAY / TIME]

> "Anything I didn't ask that you think we should know?"
