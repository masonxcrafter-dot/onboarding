# 01 — Sale Closed & Client Welcome (Phases 1–2)

## Phase 1 — Sale Closed

### Trigger
Sales call ends with the prospect agreeing to work with Valero Labs.

### Sales Rep Checklist
- [ ] Confirm package
- [ ] Confirm pricing
- [ ] Confirm setup fee
- [ ] Confirm recurring fee (amount, billing date, term)
- [ ] Confirm services included
- [ ] Confirm primary service being marketed
- [ ] Confirm service area
- [ ] Confirm start date
- [ ] Confirm the client's **ad spend** budget (paid directly to the platform, separate from our fee)
- [ ] Set expectations: onboarding needs their intake, assets, and access within **3 business days** to hit a ~14-day launch
- [ ] Send agreement
- [ ] Send payment link
- [ ] Mark opportunity as **Closed Won** in GHL
- [ ] Log sales notes in the opportunity (pain points, promises made, objections, anything the AM must know)

### 🚫 Hard Gate
**Do not move to onboarding until:**

- **Agreement = Signed**
- **Payment = Received**

If either is outstanding after 48 hours, Sales follows up. Onboarding does not begin "on good faith."

### Sales → AM Handoff
When both gates are met, Sales hands the client to the AM with:
- Package + pricing summary
- Primary service + service area
- Start date
- Sales call notes / recording
- Anything promised on the call (so the AM doesn't get surprised)

## Phase 2 — Client Welcome

### Trigger
Payment received **and** agreement signed (GHL: opportunity moves to **Onboarding → New Client**).

### AM Checklist (within 1 business day)
- [ ] Send the [Welcome Email](../templates/welcome-email.md) with the onboarding link
- [ ] Include the [Intake Form](../templates/client-intake-form.md) link
- [ ] Include the [Access Request Guide](../templates/access-request-guide.md)
- [ ] Include the [Asset Request List](../templates/asset-request-list.md) + shared upload folder link
- [ ] Include the kickoff call booking link
- [ ] Create the client folder (see [03 — Internal Client Setup](03-internal-client-setup.md))
- [ ] Set up the client communication channel
- [ ] Move opportunity to **Awaiting Intake**

### What the client should understand after the welcome
1. Complete the onboarding form
2. Send over the requested business assets
3. Give us the required account access
4. Book the kickoff call
5. We build and prepare the marketing system
6. We review everything with them before launch
