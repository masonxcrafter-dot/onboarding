# 06 — Creative Production (Phase 10)

For every client, develop multiple **angles** — not just multiple versions of the same ad.

## Angle Framework

| Angle | Purpose | Roofing example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Problem** | Call out the pain they're feeling now | "Noticed a leak after the last storm?" |
| **2. Education** | Build trust + reach earlier-stage buyers | "3 signs your roof may need to be replaced." |
| **3. Offer** | Give a reason to act now | "Free roof inspection for homeowners in [area]." |
| **4. Social Proof** | Show real work + real customers | "Here's what we just completed for a homeowner in [area]." |
| **5. Founder** | Owner speaking directly to camera | Owner explains why they do things differently |

### Launch minimum
- At least **3 angles** live at launch, with **2–3 creatives per angle** (mix of video + static/carousel).
- Hooks: write 3+ hook variations for the top video.

## Creative Checklist

Every creative needs:
- [ ] Hook
- [ ] Problem
- [ ] Solution
- [ ] Proof
- [ ] Offer
- [ ] CTA
- [ ] Correct dimensions (9:16 Stories/Reels, 4:5 Feed, 1:1 as fallback)
- [ ] Captions (most people watch on mute)
- [ ] Branding
- [ ] Contact information if necessary
- [ ] Location mentioned (city/area) — local relevance lifts response
- [ ] No claims we can't back up; financing/guarantee language approved by client

## Workflow
1. Creative drafts hooks + scripts from the Growth Brief
2. AM sends to client for approval **once** (batch all creatives into one review)
3. Edit, caption, export to `07 - Ad Creative` with naming: `Client_Angle_Format_Version` (e.g. `Summit_Offer_Reel_v1`)
4. Media Buyer confirms specs before upload
