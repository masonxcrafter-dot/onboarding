# 00 — Principles, Roles & Communication Rules

## Core Principle

> **The client isn't just hiring us for ads. They're handing us the system responsible for turning marketing into booked jobs.**

What that means in practice:

- We measure **jobs and revenue**, not just leads.
- We own the whole path: **Ad → Landing Page → Form → CRM → Instant SMS → Call/Booking → Appointment → Estimate → Job**.
- When results stall, we find **where the funnel is breaking** — even if it's in the client's sales process.
- We never generate more demand than the client can handle. **Capacity drives budget.**

## Client Communication Rules

Set these expectations **on the welcome email and again on the kickoff call.**

### Response Time
- **Business hours (Mon–Fri, 9am–5pm client local time):** we respond within **4 business hours**.
- **Urgent issues** (see below): within **1 business hour**.
- **After hours / weekends:** next business morning, unless urgent.

> Adjust the numbers to what the team can actually hold. A promise we keep beats a faster one we break.

### One Point of Contact

Keep communication centralized. The client should never be texting five different people.

```text
Client → Account Manager → Internal Team
```

- The client has **one channel** (e.g. a shared Slack Connect channel, GHL conversation, or a single email thread) and **one named AM**.
- Internal team members do not take requests directly from the client — they route them to the AM.

## Client Request System

Every client request gets categorized **before** anyone works on it.

| Priority | Examples | Target response | Target resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🔴 **Urgent** | Campaign broken · Leads not being received · Tracking broken · Major technical issue | 1 business hour | Same day |
| 🟡 **Normal** | Creative revision · New offer · Landing page change | 4 business hours | 2–3 business days |
| 🟢 **Future** | New funnel · New service · Additional campaign · New automation | Acknowledge within 1 business day | Scoped in next weekly call / 30-day review |

**Why:** this keeps the team from constantly getting pulled off task, and it turns 🟢 requests into natural **upsell and expansion** conversations.

### Request intake flow
1. Client sends request to AM.
2. AM tags it 🔴 / 🟡 / 🟢 and creates a task with owner + due date.
3. AM confirms receipt and expected timing to the client.
4. Owner completes; AM confirms with client and closes the task.
