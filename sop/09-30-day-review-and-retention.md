## Identify the Bottleneck

Our job is to find **where the funnel is breaking.**

| Pattern | Likely bottleneck | What we do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low leads, high CPL | Offer / creative / targeting | New angles, offer test, audience changes |
| **100 leads → 10 appointments** | Speed-to-lead / contact / qualification — marketing may not be the only problem | Audit response time, tighten automations, qualifying questions on form |
| Appointments booked, low show rate | Confirmation + reminders | Add reminders, confirmation call, reduce booking lead time |
| **10 appointments → 8 estimates → 1 job** | Sales process | Estimate follow-up sequence, sales process recommendations, financing emphasis |
| Good close rate, capacity full | Success — ready to scale | Raise prices / add service line / expand area |

## Retention Checklist
- [ ] 30-day review meeting held (30 minutes, AM + Media Buyer)
- [ ] Before vs. current table completed
- [ ] Bottleneck identified and agreed with the client
- [ ] Next 30/60/90-day growth plan presented
- [ ] Upsell opportunities discussed (from 🟢 Future requests, new services, new areas, new channels)
- [ ] Ask for a testimonial / referral if results are strong
- [ ] Opportunity moved to **Retained / Scale** in the client-success pipeline

## Ongoing Cadence After Day 30
- Weekly report + weekly call (can move to bi-weekly calls once stable)
- Monthly performance review
- Quarterly strategy review (new angles, new offers, seasonality, budget changes)
