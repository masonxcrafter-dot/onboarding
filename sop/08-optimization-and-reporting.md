# 08 — Optimization & Weekly Reporting (Phases 14–16)

## Phase 14 — First 7 Days

Don't immediately judge the entire campaign. Let data come in; fix what's broken, don't over-edit what's working.

### Monitor daily
- Spend
- Leads
- CPL
- Lead quality
- Contact rate
- Appointment rate
- Sales feedback

### Questions to answer
- Are people responding to the offer?
- Are leads qualified?
- Are leads answering calls?
- Are appointments being booked?
- Is the contractor following up?

### Review checkpoints
| Day | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Day 1** | Delivery — is spend pacing, are leads arriving in CRM, are automations firing? |
| **Day 3** | Early signals — CTR, CPL trend, first lead-quality feedback from client |
| **Day 7** | First real read — which angles are winning, contact + appointment rates, first optimizations |

Log each review in the client's task list with: what we saw → what we changed → why.

## Phase 15 — Weekly Report

Template: [`templates/weekly-report.md`](../templates/weekly-report.md)

The report answers **five questions**:

1. **What happened?** → e.g. 62 leads generated
2. **How much did it cost?** → e.g. $31 CPL
3. **What happened to those leads?** → e.g. 28 appointments booked
4. **What happened to the appointments?** → e.g. 18 estimates completed
5. **What happened to the estimates?** → e.g. 7 jobs closed

Then: **Revenue generated: $XX,XXX** and **ROAS / cost per job.**

Deliver on the same day every week (e.g. Monday by noon), saved to `09 - Reports`.

## Phase 16 — Weekly Client Call

- **Length:** 15–20 minutes
- **Agenda:** [`templates/weekly-call-agenda.md`](../templates/weekly-call-agenda.md)

**Opening**
> "Let's quickly go through what happened this week, what we're seeing, and what we're changing."

**Review:** leads · CPL · appointments · show rate · estimates · close rate · revenue · lead quality

**Ask the client**
- "How did the lead quality feel this week?"
- "Any common objections?"
- "Any services you're getting a lot of requests for?"
- "How's the follow-up process going?"

**Close:** explain next week's plan + any action items for the client.
