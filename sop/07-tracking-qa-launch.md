# 07 — Tracking, QA & Launch (Phases 11–13)

## Phase 11 — Tracking

Before launch:
- [ ] Meta Pixel installed on landing + thank-you pages
- [ ] Conversions API (CAPI) connected where possible
- [ ] Conversion events configured (Lead, Schedule, and later Purchase/closed job)
- [ ] UTM tracking on every ad
- [ ] Form tracking (UTMs captured into CRM fields)
- [ ] Call tracking number(s) set up
- [ ] CRM attribution — source recorded on every contact
- [ ] Appointment tracking
- [ ] Revenue tracking — client marks jobs Won with job value

The goal is eventually to answer:

> **"How much revenue did this campaign actually generate?"**

Not just: *"How many leads did we get?"*

---

## Phase 12 — QA

Run the full [Launch QA Checklist](../checklists/launch-qa-checklist.md). It covers:

- **Funnel** — page works, mobile, form, calendar, thank-you page
- **CRM** — lead enters, correct stage, SMS fires, internal notification fires, correct assignment
- **Ads** — correct account, location, budget, audience, creative, CTA, tracking
- **End-to-end test** — submit a fake lead, call the number, book a test appointment, confirm every automation

### 🚫 Hard Gate
**Do not launch until everything passes.** QA is signed off by someone who did **not** build it.

---

## Phase 13 — Launch

### Launch checklist
- [ ] Test leads/appointments deleted from CRM + calendar
- [ ] Campaign published and approved by the platform
- [ ] Client notified with the [Launch Email](../templates/launch-email.md)
- [ ] Client confirmed who is responding to leads + has the GHL app notifications on
- [ ] Opportunity moved to **Live**
- [ ] First 7-day monitoring scheduled (Day 1 / 3 / 7 reviews)

**The single most important message to the client at launch:**

> **Respond to new leads as quickly as possible.** (Target: under 5 minutes.)
