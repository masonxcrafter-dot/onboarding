# 02 — Intake, Access & Asset Collection (Phase 3)

The goal of this phase: **collect everything we need once**, so we can build without repeatedly bothering the client.

Build the form in **GoHighLevel, Typeform, Tally, or another form builder**. The full question set is in [`templates/client-intake-form.md`](../templates/client-intake-form.md).

## Intake Form Sections

| # | Section | Why it matters |
| - | --- | --- |
| 1 | Business Information | Basics, service area, team size |
| 2 | Services | What to market, job value, **capacity** |
| 3 | Offer | What gives someone a reason to contact them *now* |
| 4 | Ideal Customer | Targeting, messaging, creative angles |
| 5 | Sales Process | Where leads get lost after we deliver them |
| 6 | Current Marketing | Baseline numbers + what's already been tried |
| 7 | Access Request | Accounts we need permissions to |
| 8 | Asset Collection | Brand, photos, video, social proof |

### Capacity is non-negotiable
> *"How many additional jobs can you handle per month?"*

We don't generate 200 leads for a company that can only handle 20 jobs. Capacity sets the ceiling on budget and lead volume.

### Don't default to a discount
For high-ticket contractors the offer is often **not** a discount. Strong options:

- Free Estimate
- Free Inspection
- Same-Day Quote
- Priority Scheduling
- Financing Available
- Limited-Service Promotion

- [ ] Google Business Profile
- [ ] Website (CMS user)
- [ ] Domain / DNS (for landing page subdomain + email authentication)
- [ ] GoHighLevel (sub-account)
- [ ] CRM (if not GHL)
- [ ] Calendar
- [ ] Call tracking
- [ ] Analytics (GA4)
- [ ] Tag Manager (GTM)

### 🔒 Security Rule
**Never ask the client to send passwords through email or a normal form.**
Use proper account invitations, partner requests, and user permissions. If a platform genuinely has no user-invite option, use a password manager share (e.g. 1Password / Bitwarden shared item) — never plain text.

## Asset Collection

Create a shared upload folder per client (see [client folder structure](03-internal-client-setup.md#client-folder-structure)) and send the client the [Asset Request List](../templates/asset-request-list.md).

- **Brand:** logo, brand guidelines, colors, fonts
- **Business:** team, trucks, equipment, office, job-site, before/after, finished projects
- **Social proof:** testimonials, Google reviews, video testimonials, case studies
- **Video:** vertical phone footage, job-site, before/after, owner talking-head, customer testimonials, team, work being performed

> Raw phone footage beats polished stock every time for contractor ads. Tell the client: *"Don't overthink it — just film the work."*

## Follow-Up Cadence (AM owns this)

Missing intake, access, or assets is the #1 reason launches slip.

| When | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Day 1 (welcome sent) | Welcome email + onboarding link |
| Day 2 | Friendly SMS check-in: "Did you get the onboarding link?" |
| Day 3 | Email listing exactly what's still missing |
| Day 4 | Phone call from the AM |
| Day 5+ | Every business day: SMS/call until complete. Offer to **fill the form together on a call**. |
| Day 7 | If still blocked: flag internally, tell client the launch date is moving and why |

**Tip:** If the client is stuck on access, get on a 10-minute screen-share and walk them through it. Faster than 5 emails.

### Phase exit criteria
- [ ] Intake form submitted
- [ ] All **required** access granted and verified (we logged in and confirmed permission level)
- [ ] Minimum assets received: logo + at least 10 job photos + at least 3 reviews/testimonials
- [ ] Kickoff call booked
