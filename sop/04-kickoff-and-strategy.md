# 04 — Kickoff Call & Strategy (Phases 5–6)

## Phase 5 — Kickoff Call

- **Duration:** 30–45 minutes
- **Who:** AM (leads), Media Buyer (optional but recommended), client owner + whoever handles leads/sales
- **Objective:** Get enough information to build the marketing system without repeatedly bothering the client.

### Before the call
- [ ] Read the intake form end to end
- [ ] Read the sales notes
- [ ] Review their website, Google Business Profile, reviews, and any existing ads (Meta Ad Library)
- [ ] Note gaps/contradictions in the intake to clarify

### Call flow
Full script: [`templates/kickoff-call-script.md`](../templates/kickoff-call-script.md)

1. **Business** — what they do, what makes money, job value, capacity, target areas
2. **Customer** — best customers, problems, why they call, objections
3. **Offer** — what gives a ready buyer a reason to contact them *right now*
4. **Sales** — map **Lead → Contact → Qualification → Appointment → Estimate → Close**
5. **Goals** — *"If we look back 90 days from now, what would make you say this was absolutely worth it?"* (write their answer down verbatim)
6. **Expectations** — communication rules, response times, what we need from them (fast lead response), launch date

### After the call (same day)
- [ ] Call notes + recording saved to `10 - Strategy`
- [ ] Any missing assets/access re-requested
- [ ] Opportunity moved to **Strategy**

## Phase 6 — Internal Strategy

After the kickoff, the team creates the **Client Growth Brief** using [`templates/growth-brief.md`](../templates/growth-brief.md).

### Growth Brief must define
- Client · Primary Service · Location · Average Job Value · Capacity
- Target Customer (ICP) · Core Problem · Offer · Primary CTA · Main Differentiator
- Marketing Channel · Budget · Ad Angles · Funnel Plan · KPIs and targets

### Approval
- [ ] AM + Media Buyer sign off on the brief
- [ ] Brief shared with Creative and Automation — this is the single source of truth for the build
- [ ] Opportunity moved to **Build**
